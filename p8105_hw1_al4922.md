`The penguins dataset describes the various characteristics (habitat and various physical characteristics) of three species of penguins Adelie, Chinstrap and Gentoo. Its key variables are: species, island, bill lengths and depth, body mass, flipper length, year and sex. The data includes 344 individuals and 8 distinct variables and the mean flipper length of the penguins is 200.9 mm.`
